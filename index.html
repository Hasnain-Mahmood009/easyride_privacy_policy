Privacy Policy

How EasyRide handles personal and service information

This Privacy Policy explains how HH Labs (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information in the EasyRide Android application, a Pakistan intercity ride-scheduling and seat-booking service.

By creating an account or using EasyRide, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. If you do not agree with the practices described here, please do not use the application.

Important: EasyRide is a ride-scheduling and seat-booking platform. It is not a continuous location-tracking application. Location search and autocomplete operate offline on your device as described below.

Scope & Application Details

Who operates EasyRide and who may use it

  • Application: EasyRide — Pakistan Intercity Ride-Scheduling & Seat Booking App.
  • Developer: HH Labs, Karak, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Pakistan.
  • Android package identifier: com.aistudio.karakrides.k8v2p.
  • Support contact: easyridesupportteam@gmail.com.

This Policy applies to information processed through the EasyRide application and its described Firebase-backed services. It does not automatically cover third-party websites or services that may be linked from the application; those services have their own privacy terms.

Information We Collect

Information needed to operate bookings, accounts, and safety checks

Account & Profile Information

Full legal name, Pakistani mobile phone number in +92 format verified through Firebase Phone Authentication OTP, optional email address, and account role: PASSENGER or DRIVER.

Driver Verification Documents

For drivers only: full legal name, registered phone number, CNIC number, Driving License number, and vehicle registration number and details, including vehicle name, model/type, and AC availability. These details are submitted for manual administrator verification.

Ride, Booking & Schedule Data

Trip origin and destination cities or locations, departure date and time, seat availability, fare details, pickup and drop points, booking receipts, and booking or cancellation history.

Device, Diagnostics & Notifications

Firebase Crashlytics crash logs, Firebase Analytics usage diagnostics, network connectivity state, and Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens used to deliver ride and booking status alerts.

Information submitted by drivers

Driver verification information is requested only when a user chooses to register or operate as a driver. It is used for manual review and safety-related eligibility checks. Do not submit another person's identity documents or information without lawful authority.

Information generated through use

When you create, update, or cancel a ride or booking, the related schedule and transaction records are processed so that passengers and drivers can coordinate the requested trip. Booking and cancellation records may be retained for support, dispute handling, safety, and fraud-prevention purposes.

How We Use Information

Information is used to provide and protect the EasyRide service

We use the information described in this Policy for the following service-related purposes:

  • Authenticate accounts and verify Pakistani mobile numbers through SMS one-time passwords (OTP).
  • Review driver identity, driving licence, and vehicle details to support passenger safety.
  • Create, manage, process, and cancel ride schedules and seat bookings.
  • Display trip details and coordinate pickup, drop-off, fares, and seat availability.
  • Send ride, booking, cancellation, and status notifications through Firebase Cloud Messaging.
  • Respond to support requests, investigate reported issues, and maintain service records.
  • Diagnose crashes, understand application stability, and improve the reliability of EasyRide.
  • Apply account moderation, safety warnings, and fraud-prevention measures where necessary.

We do not use driver verification documents for advertising. We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties or advertisers.

Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Firebase services used to operate the application

EasyRide uses Google Firebase services as backend and infrastructure processors. Information may be transmitted to and processed by the relevant Firebase service providers to perform the functions listed below:

Firebase Authentication
Account authentication and phone-number verification using SMS OTP.

Cloud Firestore
Cloud storage and synchronization for account, driver, ride, schedule, and booking records.

Firebase Crashlytics
Crash reports and diagnostic information used to identify application failures.

Firebase Analytics
Application usage diagnostics and interaction information used to understand stability and improve the service.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)
Push notification delivery for ride, booking, and status alerts using device registration tokens.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to valid legal process, protect users, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, or enforce our Terms. We do not sell or rent personal data to third parties or advertisers.

Google's handling of information in its services is also governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy documentation. Please review Google's privacy information for details about its processing practices.

Location Search & Device Permissions

Offline location lookup; no continuous background GPS tracking

Offline location search: EasyRide's city/location search and autocomplete run 100% offline on the user's device using a bundled Pakistan locations dataset named locations.json.

The offline search feature uses the bundled dataset to suggest and match locations. It does not require a live location lookup service and does not continuously track the device's GPS location in the background.

Trip origin, destination, pickup, and drop-off details that you actively enter or select are ride and booking information. These manually supplied trip details are distinct from a device's continuous GPS location.

Data Storage & Security

Cloud records, local cache, and reasonable safeguards

EasyRide stores application data in Google Cloud Firestore and may maintain a local Room database cache on the user's device to support application functionality. Information transmitted between the application and supported backend services is encrypted in transit using TLS/HTTPS.

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or loss. Access to driver verification information is intended to be limited to authorized administrative review and service-related needs.

No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. Although we take reasonable steps to protect information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users should protect access to their devices, phone numbers, and accounts.

Data Retention, Moderation & Safety

Keeping records only as needed for service and legitimate purposes

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide EasyRide, maintain account and booking records, complete driver verification, respond to support requests, resolve disputes, enforce platform rules, and meet applicable legal or safety requirements.

When an account deletion request is verified, we aim to delete or de-identify associated personal information within 7 to 30 days, subject to the exceptions described in the Account & Data Deletion section. Some limited records may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, or the establishment or defence of legal claims.

EasyRide may apply account moderation, safety warnings, restrictions, or suspension where activity appears to violate platform rules, create a safety concern, or indicate suspected fraud or misuse.

Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your EasyRide account and associated data

You may request deletion of your EasyRide account and associated personal information, including your registered phone number, trip and booking history, and driver verification records, by contacting HH Labs from the support email below.

Request account deletion

  1. Send an email to easyridesupportteam@gmail.com using the subject line “EasyRide Account and Data Deletion Request”.
  2. Include the Pakistani mobile number registered with your EasyRide account in +92 format.
  3. State that you want your EasyRide account and associated personal data deleted. If you are a driver, explicitly include a request to delete your driver verification records.
  4. Respond to any reasonable identity-verification questions needed to confirm that you own the account.
  5. We will review the request and send a confirmation or ask for additional information if required.
Deletion timeline: After successful verification, we aim to complete deletion within 7 to 30 days.

Where associated with your account and subject to lawful retention exceptions, a deletion request covers:

  • Your EasyRide account and profile details, including your name, registered phone number, optional email address, and user role.
  • Your trip, ride schedule, seat booking, pickup/drop-off, fare, receipt, and cancellation history.
  • Your driver verification details, including CNIC, driving licence, and vehicle registration information.
  • Associated device notification registration information and personal data held in the service systems.

Information stored in local Room cache may be cleared as part of account removal or when the application data is cleared from your device. You may also uninstall EasyRide and clear its local app data from your device after submitting a request.

Limited information may be retained where required by applicable law or where reasonably necessary for fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution, enforcement of platform rules, or the establishment or defence of legal claims. Retained information will be restricted to the relevant purpose and handled in accordance with applicable requirements.

Account deletion and privacy supporteasyridesupportteam@gmail.com

Please note: A request is not considered verified until we have confirmed that it relates to the account holder. The stated 7–30 day period begins after successful verification.
